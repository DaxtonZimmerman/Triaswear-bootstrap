OUR STANDARD

We take quality seriously.

Every product goes through more than just making a graphic and placing it on a shirt.

01

Design

Graphics, typography, sizing, and placement are revised before a design becomes a finished product.

02

Samples

We order physical samples so we can see how the clothing, print, and design actually look in person.

03

Final Review

We check the final appearance and consistency before deciding a product is ready to represent TriasWear.

BEHIND THE BRAND

01

02

03
